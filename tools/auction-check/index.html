Apne ad ka check (2 minute)

Ads Manager → Ads tab → Columns → Customise columns → Quality ranking, Engagement rate ranking, Conversion rate ranking. Jo dikhe woh yahan select karo. (500 se kam impressions wale ads pe ranking nahi aati.)

Quality ranking

Aapka ad, same audience ke competing ads ke muqable kitna quality wala lagta hai

Engagement rate ranking

Expected engagement, competing ads ke muqable

Conversion rate ranking

Expected conversion rate, same optimisation goal wale ads ke muqable

Diagnosis

Yaad rakhne wali baatein

  1. Ranking sirf tab dekho jab ad aapka goal miss kar raha ho. Achha perform karne wale ad ki ranking low ho, toh bhi theek hai.
  2. Low se average lana, average se above average lane se zyada faydemand hai. Pehle "below average" wali ranking fix karo.
  3. Perfect creative ya perfect audience nahi, sahi creative + audience ka fit dhoondho.
  4. Clickbait, engagement bait aur sensational wording se quality girti hai.
